Our approach

Care, curated.

Kensington was founded on a simple belief: care in the home should feel personal, private, and quietly excellent. We take on a limited number of families, hand-select each caregiver, and coordinate every detail — so daily life feels lighter, and dignity is never in question.

A consultation is an unhurried conversation, not a sales call. We listen for the shape of the day — the routines, the preferences, the things that matter and go unsaid — and design support that sits quietly inside the life already there.

What we provide

A complete circle of support.

Non-medical companion and personal care for life at home. Every offering is practical, discreet, and designed to preserve independence — never clinical treatment.

For caregivers

We are building a small, exceptional team.

Kensington hires slowly and deliberately. We are looking for companion and personal-care professionals with real experience, unhurried manners, and the discretion that private households require.
